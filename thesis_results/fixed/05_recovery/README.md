# Fixed route: posterior recovery

Coverage and recovery error, posterior covariance eigen-analysis, feature reconstruction and downstream error budget, and the second-mode check of one cell; the combinations of the parameters that the posteriors determine, and the compensation behind them.

The eigen-analysis compares the tight and the sloppy two-dimensional subspaces of the posterior covariance (Tight2DSubspaceSimilarity and Sloppy2DSubspaceSimilarity in data/eigen_analysis/cross_scenario_comparison.csv and replicate_eigensystem_stability.csv; the figures geometry_<scenario>_tight2d_similarity and geometry_<scenario>_sloppy2d_similarity). With four parameters the two subspaces are orthogonal complements of each other, so their similarities coincide: the two columns agree in every row to rounding (at most 2e-15; both NaN where replicate 2 is missing), and the two figures of a scenario show the same values.

The feature reconstruction normalizes the error of every feature by its range over the 61 complete external cases of the fixed dataset, computed from their COMSOL pressures: FeatureScale = maximum - minimum of the pressure, ratio, drop or difference over those cases, one value per feature for every design and scenario, and no normalized error where the range is numerically zero (NormalizationUnavailableReason). data/feature_reconstruction/feature_registry.csv lists every scale with its minimum, maximum and cases. The surrogate error budget (error_budget/) does not depend on the posteriors: one table, raw_output_summary.csv.

The parameter combinations (the files starting with `combinations_` and the case outputs in `combinations/`, step `combinations`): for the cells of the settings (A.12; thesis: the 20 cases in Measurement + GP and GP only, all_six), each replicate on its own (never pooled), which combinations of the four parameters the posterior determines. In the prior-standardized coordinates of the eigen-analysis, z = (parameter - prior mean) / prior SD, a combination w'z of unit length has prior SD 1, and its SD ratio is its posterior SD: 1 when the measurement leaves it at its prior width. The tightest eigen-direction of the four parameters is the best-determined combination; for the weak pair m3_e_RD and log10_k0_RD the best- and worst-determined combinations come from their 2 x 2 block of the posterior covariance, next to the individual SD ratios, and also in log form, a ln(m3_e_RD) + b ln(k0_RD), the power law m3_e_RD^a k0_RD^b, where the draws allow it (settings A.12; otherwise the reason is given). The marginal of each best combination is computed from the projected draws against its prior (a prior sample, A.12) and the truth; the projected draws are not stored (the shipped draws give them again).

"Identified combination" marks a best weak-pair combination whose SD ratio is at most half the smaller individual SD ratio (factor in A.12): a description, not a statistical test. The best combination in standardized units depends on the prior ranges; the log form does not depend on units. The truths are synthetic, under the thesis prior. A combination the measurement determines well is necessary but not sufficient for a clinical descriptor, which needs clinical data.

The compensation, per cell at the truth: the Jacobian of the six GP means in the same coordinates, whitened by the scenario's observation covariance (as in the design study, A.8): the angle between the sensitivity vectors of m3_e_RD and log10_k0_RD (near 180 degrees: a change of one is undone by a change of the other in the same direction), the collinearity index of Brun, Reichert and Kuensch (2001) of the pair and of all four parameters (1: independent effects; large: nearly collinear), and the pair's least-informative direction from the local information of the pair alone (the other two parameters at the truth), with its angle to the worst-determined posterior direction. Across the cells of a scenario: the consensus of the best combinations (the leading eigenvector of their mean projector) and each cell's angle to it.

Posterior-based results show every cell with a selected provider together: the certified cells (two independent AIES ensembles that agree) and the healthy fallbacks (a healthy provider that was not certified). Unavailable cells have no posterior and stay blank. The index names, for each output, the cells it shows and which of them are healthy fallbacks or unavailable; a figure of one case gives its status in the title. Summary tables give next to each value its value over the certified cells alone (columns ending in `_certified_only`).

The status of every cell: [`cell_status.csv`](../04_inversion/tables/cell_status.csv).

## Full-size intermediates not shipped

A run of this analysis also writes the intermediate outputs below into its `data/` folder. thesis_results/ does not ship them because of their size; the command regenerates them at full size in the run folder (runtime on one workstation).

| File in `data/` | Holds | Regenerate with | Runtime |
|---|---|---|---|
| `coverage_recovery_summary.mat` | coverage and recovery summaries of every cell (the tables in tables/ come from it) | `run_fixed_study('Mode','rerun','Steps',{'recovery'})` | minutes to about 1 h (evaluates the GP at posterior draws) |
| `eigen_analysis/posterior_eigendecomposition_summary.mat` | the posterior covariance eigensystems of every cell and replicate pair | `run_fixed_study('Mode','rerun','Steps',{'recovery'})` | minutes to about 1 h (evaluates the GP at posterior draws) |
| `feature_reconstruction/error_budget/downstream_error_budget_long_form.csv` | the downstream error budget of every case, feature and evaluation space | `run_fixed_study('Mode','rerun','Steps',{'recovery'})` | minutes to about 1 h (evaluates the GP at posterior draws) |
