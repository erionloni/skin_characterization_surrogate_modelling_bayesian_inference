# Fixed route: Bayesian inversion

Certification status of the 360 canonical cells (20 truths x 3 scenarios x 6 designs: 230 certified, 47 healthy non-certified fallbacks, 83 unavailable) in the campaign's status table, the posterior draws of every cell with a provider, the replicate-pair diagnostics (one pair per cell, at the level that decided its status), and their ensemble bulk ESS recomputed with ranks per parameter (a warning diagnostic that no certification decision depends on).

The replicate-pair diagnostics (data/diagnostics/pair_reliability_diagnostics.csv; the figures <scenario>_pair_* draw its all_six cells) hold one pair per cell: the pair at the level that decided the cell's status (2,000 or 5,000 steps); the failed 2,000-step pairs of cells that went on to 5,000 steps are not in it. 302 of the 360 canonical cells have a pair: the 230 certified, 23 healthy fallbacks whose pair did not certify after 5,000 steps, and 49 unavailable cells. The other 24 healthy fallbacks have none, because their second replicate ran out of time (TIMEOUT in the status table); their posterior is the healthy replicate (ProviderReplicate). The file also holds the pairs of 79 of the 80 conditional cells of A-D (Campaign conditional); the 80 cells of E and F keep their certification record in their draw files (fixed/07_conditional_inversion/data/draws).

Posterior-based results show every cell with a selected provider together: the certified cells (two independent AIES ensembles that agree) and the healthy fallbacks (a healthy provider that was not certified). Unavailable cells have no posterior and stay blank. The index names, for each output, the cells it shows and which of them are healthy fallbacks or unavailable; a figure of one case gives its status in the title. Summary tables give next to each value its value over the certified cells alone (columns ending in `_certified_only`).

The status of every cell: [`cell_status.csv`](tables/cell_status.csv).
