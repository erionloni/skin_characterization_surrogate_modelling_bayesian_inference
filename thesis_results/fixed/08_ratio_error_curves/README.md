# Fixed route: ratio-error curves

How well the parameter values inside each posterior reproduce the observed cycle-to-cycle pressure ratios: the ratio discrepancy d and the four ratio errors of the GP mean at every draw, along each parameter (Measurement + GP, all six outputs, 20 cases; case 113 in GP only). Descriptive: the ratios are functions of the same six pressures and add no information.

d combines the errors of the two ratios of each probe (cycles 2 and 3 over cycle 1) weighted for their shared cycle-1 denominator, in units of an 8 % relative error (settings 0.7): d = 1 when the ratios are off by about one such error; the level discrepancy measures the overall pressure level instead. A flat curve: the ratios give that parameter no leverage within the posterior. A structured curve (stable across bins and replicates) gives leverage only if ratio errors are more precise than independent 8 % errors imply, an unmeasured property of the device. The observations are exact COMSOL outputs, so structure near the truth can look more decisive than it would with real, noisy data. The curves show association along plausible parameter combinations, not causation.

Posterior-based results show every cell with a selected provider together: the certified cells (two independent AIES ensembles that agree) and the healthy fallbacks (a healthy provider that was not certified). Unavailable cells have no posterior and stay blank. The index names, for each output, the cells it shows and which of them are healthy fallbacks or unavailable; a figure of one case gives its status in the title. Summary tables give next to each value its value over the certified cells alone (columns ending in `_certified_only`).

The status of every cell: [`cell_status.csv`](../04_inversion/tables/cell_status.csv).

- `draws/` (42 files): per cell and replicate, in the row order of the cell's posterior draws: the draw index, walker, retained iteration and sampler step, the ratio discrepancy d, the level discrepancy and the four log-ratio errors e1..e4 (full precision; the GP means are not stored: the shipped draws and model give them again)
- files: `bin_summaries.csv`, `cohort.csv`, `eta2.csv`, `truth_markers.csv`
