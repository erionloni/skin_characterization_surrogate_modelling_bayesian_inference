# Fixed route: design study

Fisher information, posterior covariance and ranking of the six observation designs per scenario.

The ranking tables rank the designs by the mean weak-pair information (RankByQInformation, the certified-only rank alongside); their other means (full information, D-utility, posterior SD, contraction, coverage) give their own orders. ConditionNumber (data/scenarios/<scenario>/tables/fim_metrics.csv) is s_1/s_4 of the whitened Jacobian, the square root of the condition number of the Fisher information, and Inf for a design with fewer than four outputs: d2_only and d6_only leave a one-dimensional null space, cycle_1_only a two-dimensional one (MeanNumericalRank in the ranking tables).

The information scores from the posterior draws are Monte Carlo estimates of covariance moments. For the nested designs (cycle 1, D2 or D6 followed by all six; sequential_information.csv) 9 of the 60 steps of Measurement + GP gain negative weak-pair information (QIncrement down to -0.118 nats), and 54 of them have a covariance reduction with a negative eigenvalue (MinimumPSDReductionEigenvalue down to -0.022; GP only 28 of 60, down to -0.040): Monte Carlo and moment error, not information lost by adding pressures.

Posterior-based results show every cell with a selected provider together: the certified cells (two independent AIES ensembles that agree) and the healthy fallbacks (a healthy provider that was not certified). Unavailable cells have no posterior and stay blank. The index names, for each output, the cells it shows and which of them are healthy fallbacks or unavailable; a figure of one case gives its status in the title. Summary tables give next to each value its value over the certified cells alone (columns ending in `_certified_only`).

The status of every cell: [`cell_status.csv`](../04_inversion/tables/cell_status.csv).
