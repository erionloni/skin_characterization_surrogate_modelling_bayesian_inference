# Fixed route: surrogates

The six Matern-3/2 Gaussian-process and PCE surrogates of the final production run (output-6 seed 221754153), their assessment on the 61 external cases with the calibrated emulator variance (gamma 0.302) and the development audit (kernels, seeds, cross-validation, calibration); how well the GP and PCE means reproduce 26 derived features of the pressures on the external cases.

The hyperparameter table (tables/gp_hyperparameters.csv) flags each length scale theta at the upper bound 10 of UQLab's optimization, whose default bounds the fits keep (AtUpperBound_<input>: theta above the value of settings 0.4; thesis: 9 of the 24 length scales). The process variance sigma^2 is optimized together with the length scales between (0.1 SD)^2 and (10 SD)^2 of the training outputs; the nugget is a fixed noise variance.

sigma^2, and with it the native predictive variance, is not identified in scale: the same-data refits of the audit change it by factors of 4.5 to 1,494 (Matern-3/2) while the D6 means move by less than 0.003 mbar. The calibration v = tau^2 h^gamma works on h = v_native / sigma^2, which removes that scale, so scores of the raw variance do not rank kernels. gamma 0.302 is the mean of 500 case-cluster bootstrap estimates, each the best value on a grid from 0 to 1 in steps of 0.025, so it lies off the grid; the bootstrap's 95 % interval (0.225 to 0.375) can only take grid values.

The feature accuracy (the files starting with `feature_accuracy_`, step `feature_accuracy`): the six pressures and every feature of the feature catalog of the settings (0.7), computed from the GP mean and from the PCE mean, against the same feature computed from the COMSOL pressures, on the 61 external cases only (the predictions that the surrogate assessment saved; no external case was used for training, selection or calibration). Point predictions, not posteriors; nothing is tuned on the result. Log-linear features (ratios, levels, the curvature s, the pressures) are compared in log: e = log f(mean) - log f(COMSOL), about the relative error; differences in mbar, drop ratios raw. Per feature: the bias with mean +/- 1.96 SD / sqrt(n), RMSE and R^2 with 95 % case-bootstrap intervals (settings 0.7), MAE, median and maximum |e|, and the five largest errors with their inputs.

Two scales put an error in context. The spread, the SD of the COMSOL feature over the external cases, is the signal: R^2 = 1 - sum(e^2) / sum((f - mean f)^2) and NRMSE = RMSE / spread. Ratios vary little between tissues, so a lower R^2 means a harder target, not a worse GP: read RMSE next to R^2. The noise scale is descriptive: the SD of the feature under independent errors of the reference CV on the six pressures (settings 0.7; 0.113 for a within-probe ratio, 0.160 for R21; the delta method on the COMSOL pressures for a raw feature, median over the cases); the device's noise and its correlations are not measured. The rounding SD: the closing pressures are registered on a 1 s time grid (the COMSOL solver's steps; data/README.md), so the gridded outputs (settings 0.7: pcl1_D2_mbar and pcl1_D6_mbar, on the grid in most cases) carry a 4 mbar step (0.4 kPa/s); uniform rounding alone gives the reported SD of the feature (median over the cases), a part of its error against COMSOL that is not GP error. pcl2_D2_mbar and pcl3_D2_mbar lie on the grid in a minority of the cases and are not counted. No value is corrected.

The correlation of the relative errors delta = log(mean / COMSOL) between the six outputs explains every log-linear feature at once: Var(e) = w' Sigma_delta w, the variance terms sum w_k^2 Var(delta_k) plus the covariance terms. Negative covariance terms (errors of a ratio's pressures correlated positively) mean that the errors cancel in the feature. The emulator check: z = e / s with s = sqrt(sum w_k^2 v_k / mu_k^2) from the calibrated emulator variances v under the likelihood's assumption of independent GP errors (GP, log-linear features). SD(z) < 1: s overstates the feature's errors, the assumption is conservative for it; SD(z) > 1: s understates them.

A drop ratio whose denominator changes sign over the external cases, or whose most extreme case carries more than the share of the variance in the settings (0.7; the rule of the feature sensitivity), is UNSTABLE: reported and drawn in its parity panel, left out of the error strip (in the thesis results: dropratio_D6). The rankings of the feature sensitivity (03_sensitivity) show each feature's R^2 and NRMSE from here.
