# Confirmation

The confirmation experiment of the settings (B.7): 20 canonical truths x 4 protocols (the default and three objective-focused protocols), 160 D2/D6 COMSOL solves; its inversions (measurement_plus_gp, observation design d2_only: the three D2 pressures; 80 cells: 73 certified, 7 without an accepted observation) and the 60 paired comparisons with the shared default (51 with both posteriors); all_six on the 6 complete pairs only (9 cells, certified), as a description.

The parameter combinations of the confirmation (the files starting with `combinations_` and the case outputs in `combinations/`, step `combinations`): the analysis of the fixed route's recovery (`fixed/05_recovery`, files `combinations_`) on the confirmation's posteriors, per material, protocol, observation design and replicate (never pooled), in the prior-standardized coordinates z = (parameter - prior mean) / prior SD of the four material parameters: the best- and worst-determined combinations of the weak pair m3_e_RD and log10_k0_RD from their 2 x 2 block of the posterior covariance, next to the individual SD ratios, also in log form (the power law m3_e_RD^a k0_RD^b) where the draws allow it, the marginal of each best combination against its prior and the truth, and the consensus of the materials per protocol (settings B.8). "Identified combination": a description, not a statistical test.

What a focused protocol changes (`combinations_protocols`, `combinations_protocol_summary`, the figures `combinations_<design>_protocol_effect`): on the materials with a posterior (certified or healthy fallback) under the focused protocol and under the default, per replicate, the rotation of the best weak-pair combination from the default's (the angle between the two directions, 0 to 90 degrees) and the ratio of their SD ratios (below 1: the focused protocol determines its best combination more sharply), next to the angle between the two replicates of each cell: a rotation smaller than that angle is within the run-to-run variation of the sampler. The designs are the confirmation's (B.7): the complete pairs of all six pressures are few and descriptive.

Posterior-based results show every cell with a selected provider together: the certified cells (two independent AIES ensembles that agree) and the healthy fallbacks (a healthy provider that was not certified). Unavailable cells have no posterior and stay blank. The index names, for each output, the cells it shows and which of them are healthy fallbacks or unavailable; a figure of one case gives its status in the title. Summary tables give next to each value its value over the certified cells alone (columns ending in `_certified_only`).

The status of every cell: [`cell_status.csv`](tables/cell_status.csv).

Inside `data/`:

- `campaign/` (48 files): the confirmation campaign: status, certification, summaries, cell grid and seed schedule, pairing, inputs, configuration and reports
- `combinations/` (7 files): the weak-pair combinations the confirmation's posteriors determine, per material, protocol, design and replicate (cohort.csv, cells.csv, marginals.csv, densities.csv: the marginal figures; consensus.csv), and what each focused protocol changes against the default (protocols.csv, protocol_summary.csv); the projected draws are not stored (the draws give them again)
- `design/` (1 file): what the step export_design writes: the screen's proxy of the design's protocols in the confirmation's observation designs (design_predictions.csv) and, in a rerun, the design of the settings (materials.csv, protocols.csv, design_rows.csv) and the COMSOL run list (probe_solves.csv)
- `draws/` (83 files): every retained draw of both replicates of the provider's level, at full precision, for each certified cell and healthy fallback: one file per cell, with index.csv
- `observations/` (1 file): the accepted COMSOL observations of the design (confirmation_observations.csv), the QC of every design row (observation_qc.csv), how the COMSOL solves ended per protocol and probe (comsol_outcomes.csv) and the reviewed inputs
- files: `cell_status.csv`
